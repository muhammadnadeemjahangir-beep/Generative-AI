🩺 Built an AI Health Assistant this week — meet Vita.

It's a compact chatbot widget that gives calm, plain-language answers on:
🤒 Symptoms — explained in general terms, never a diagnosis
💊 Medicines — what they're generally used for
🥗 Nutrition & lifestyle — everyday, evidence-based tips
🩹 First-aid basics — for minor, non-emergency situations
🧠 Mental health — warm support with a nudge toward real professional help

A few things I cared about while building it:

→ It never plays doctor. Red-flag symptoms (chest pain, breathing trouble, signs of stroke, suicidal thoughts) trigger a clear "seek help now" message instead of home advice.
→ No dosing advice — medicine questions get pointed to a pharmacist or doctor.
→ Every response is designed to be reassuring, not alarming.

Tech stack: Gradio for the interface, LangChain for orchestration, and Groq (Llama 3.1) for fast inference — all wrapped in a custom compact, pop-up-style UI.

This is general information only, not a medical diagnosis — always check with a qualified healthcare provider for anything urgent or persistent.

Would love feedback from anyone building in the health-tech / conversational AI space 👇
